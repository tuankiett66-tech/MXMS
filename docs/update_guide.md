
# Huong dan Su dung - Cap nhat [08/06/2026]

## 1. Luu tru du lieu & Reload trang
- Ung dung bay gio se **tu dong nho** moi thong tin ban nhap, bao gom thong tin mien giam hoc phi (50% va 100%).
- Neu ban sua "Ngay hoc chuan" thanh 25 ngay, khi bam F5 hoac tai lai trang, so 25 van se duoc giu nguyen.
- **Luu y**: Du lieu duoc luu tai may tinh/dien thoai ban dang dung. Neu ban doi sang thiet bi khac, ban can nap lai file Excel hoac sao luu.

## 2. Cach dung nut Luu (Save)
- Mac du he thong tu dong luu, ban nen bam nut **"Luu du lieu"** hoac **"Luu lai (Save)"** o Sidebar truoc khi thoat de dam bao an toan tuyet doi.

## 3. Theo doi STT (So thu tu)
- Moi hoc sinh duoc gan mot so thu tu tu dong (STT) tai tab "Hoc sinh" va "Diem danh".
- STT se tu dong danh lai tu 1 cho tung khoi de ban de dang kiem dem tong so be.

## 4. Sua thong tin be & Mien giam hoc phi
- Bam vao icon chiec viet (Edit) tren moi the hoc sinh, hoac check trực tiếp tren cac cot **Giảm 50%**, **Giảm 100%** tai bang danh sach be Excel.
- He thong tu dong tinh toan khau tru 50% hoac 100% hoc phi khi tạo phieu thu tuong ung.
- Ngay sinh se duoc hien thi dung dinh dang dd/mm/yyyy trong o chon ngay.

## 5. Cau truc file Excel Nap/Xuat chuan 100%
- Khi nhap danh sach tu file excel, ban co the them cac cot: `GIẢM 50%`, `GIẢM 100%`, `MA HS` va `MỚI` de chu dong danh dau trang thai be tu file Excel. He thong se tu dong nhan dien thong minh cac cot nay de nap hoac cap nhat truc tiep ho so be hien tai ma khong sinh trung lap.
- Khi xuat Excel, cac cot thong tin nay duoc hien thi ro rang voi dau danh dau "X" giup ban kiem soat 100% thong tin can doi.

## 6. In an & Zalo
- Tai tab Phieu thu: Dung nut In tren may tinh de co ban in 1/2 A4.
- Tren dien thoai: Bam "Sao chep & Mo Zalo" de gui nhanh thong bao hoc phi cho phu huynh.
